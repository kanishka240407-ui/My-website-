What is the Digital School Notice Board?

The Digital School Notice Board is an online platform designed to display school announcements in a clean and organised way. It works like a virtual notice board accessible to every student.

Why We Created This Website

  • To make notices accessible anytime and anywhere
  • To avoid missed or damaged physical notices
  • To reduce paper usage and support eco-friendly systems
  • To save teachers’ time by uploading once for all
  • To improve communication between school and students

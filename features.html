Website Features

📌 Daily Notice Updates

Students can get all school notices directly on the website without waiting for physical announcements. This helps everyone stay informed and reduces confusion.

📚 Exam Time-Tables

Exam schedules are uploaded in an organised format so that students can easily check their dates anytime. No more searching through WhatsApp groups or paper sheets.

🎉 Events & Activities Information

All details about cultural events, competitions, workshops, and school activities are available here. Students will never miss an event again.

📢 Important Announcements

Urgent updates like holiday notices, emergency instructions, or schedule changes are posted instantly so students can know immediately.

✨ Clean UI & Easy Navigation

The website is designed to be simple and user-friendly. All sections are easy to navigate, making it comfortable for students and teachers to use—even on mobile.
